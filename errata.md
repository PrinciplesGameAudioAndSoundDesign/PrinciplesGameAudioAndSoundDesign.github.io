## Errata

We are dedicated to making this book and the information it provides as accurate as possible, remaining consistent with the industry's best practices. As you read, we'd be grateful if you'd flag any errors or inconsistencies you come across. Please report them as a <a href="https://github.com/PrinciplesGameAudioAndSoundDesign/PGASD-2ndEdition/issues/new?labels=errata&title=Errata%20report" target="_blank">GitHub issue</a>, and we'll track and list confirmed corrections below, sorted by chapter and page number.
